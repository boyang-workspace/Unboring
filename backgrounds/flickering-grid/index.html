Usage notes

Short guidance for humans and AI tools before copying the pattern.

Use Flickering Grid when you need a grid of dots that flicker in and out at random intervals. It works best when the interface needs clarity before spectacle.

Do not make the flickering grid flashy, noisy, over-animated, or decorative for its own sake. Avoid random glow, heavy gradients, excessive borders, oversized type, unclear hierarchy, and generic SaaS filler.
