Usage notes

Short guidance for humans and AI tools before copying the pattern.

Use Dot Pattern when you need a subtle repeating dot grid for neutral surface texture. It works best when the interface needs clarity before spectacle.

Do not make the dot pattern flashy, noisy, over-animated, or decorative for its own sake. Avoid random glow, heavy gradients, excessive borders, oversized type, unclear hierarchy, and generic SaaS filler.
