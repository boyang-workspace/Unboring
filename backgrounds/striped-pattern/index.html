Usage notes

Short guidance for humans and AI tools before copying the pattern.

Use Striped Pattern when you need diagonal or horizontal stripes for accent backgrounds. It works best when the interface needs clarity before spectacle.

Do not make the striped pattern flashy, noisy, over-animated, or decorative for its own sake. Avoid random glow, heavy gradients, excessive borders, oversized type, unclear hierarchy, and generic SaaS filler.
