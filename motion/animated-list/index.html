Usage notes

Short guidance for humans and AI tools before copying the pattern.

Use Animated List when you need reveal list items with staggered entrance animations. It works best when the interface needs clarity before spectacle.

Do not make the animated list flashy, noisy, over-animated, or decorative for its own sake. Avoid random glow, heavy gradients, excessive borders, oversized type, unclear hierarchy, and generic SaaS filler.
