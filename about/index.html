About UnBoring

UnBoring helps vibe coders find product and website UI directions they can copy into prompts, CSS, or future agent workflows.

The mission

UnBoring is for people who can build but do not always know how a product should look or move. It turns design inspiration into usable directions: visual styles, motion patterns, interaction ideas, templates, prompts, negative prompts, and structured tokens.

The pain it solves

Vibe coders often spend too many tokens exploring UI ideas and still end up with generic Tailwind, shadcn, oversized hero text, card stacks, and predictable gradients.

01

Less token waste

Start from concrete design directions instead of asking AI to invent taste from vague adjectives.

02

Less template gravity

Push AI away from default SaaS dashboards and toward styles that fit the project, audience, and mood.

03

More agent-ready structure

Keep every idea copyable by humans and readable by agents through prompts, negative prompts, and tokens.

Why open source

Good design taste should not be locked inside one generator. UnBoring is open source so designers and builders can contribute styles, motion patterns, templates, and design skills that help AI-generated interfaces become more beautiful, diverse, practical, and human.
